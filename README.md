Sum up the world's news of Tuesday 24 November 2026 in one image. The program writes one image to /output/entry.png.

Main story: a factory fire at the PT Fonusa Agung Mulia candy factory in Cikande, Banten, kills 26 workers. Also in the image: Russian strikes kill at least 15 people across the Kyiv region, a tanker is involved in an incident with military forces in the Gulf of Oman, thousands march in Lisbon over living costs, Seoul names a new foreign minister and its first woman spy chief, Nvidia jumps 31.5% as Jensen Huang returns as CEO, and Carlos Alcaraz wins his first ATP Finals title while Max Verstappen wins the Las Vegas Grand Prix.

## Run it

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
```

The program uses Node 22 and the standard library only. It copies entry.png (1024 by 1024, under 1 MiB) to the output folder. It runs with no network, and two runs write the same bytes.
